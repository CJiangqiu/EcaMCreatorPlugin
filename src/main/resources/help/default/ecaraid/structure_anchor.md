How the raid finds its centre. These are mutually exclusive in ECA, which is why there is one field rather than two: **None** does not anchor at all, so the position passed to the start block becomes the centre directly and the "target destroyed" check always reports intact. **Structure** anchors to one specific structure and the centre is taken from its bounds. **Tag** anchors to any structure carrying the tag, letting one raid apply to several structure types.
